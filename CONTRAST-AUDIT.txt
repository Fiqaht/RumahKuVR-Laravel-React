RumahKuVR Contrast Audit - 2026-08-21

What was fixed
- Removed legacy light-background / light-text clashes introduced by the monochrome theme.
- Forced readable text colors on Experience, Training, Roles, Technology, Results, Contact, device cards, gallery overlays and timeline content.
- Changed gallery and gameplay captions to dark glass overlays with white text.
- Fixed the Hard difficulty card and the training/device cards so inherited dark text no longer disappears.
- Fixed architecture icon tiles so icons do not render white on a light tile.
- Made the marquee dark instead of a sudden white strip.
- Converted decorative multi-color particle text to monochrome silver/white.
- Added a dedicated white transparent RumahKuVR navbar logo for the dark theme.
- Kept elder-friendly typography sizing from the previous package.

- Body copy #C7CEC8 on #111419: 11.50:1
